CVForge

A CV that gets past the robot and interests the human

Fill in the form, pick one of six templates and print a finished A4 sheet. A separate tab checks how the document will fare in applicant tracking systems. All of it happens in your browser.

  • 01

    The preview is the print

    It is the same A4 sheet that goes to the printer, not a separate mock-up. A marker shows where the page ends before you find out on paper.

  • 02

    A check against tracking systems

    Eleven rules test what the parser and the ranking look at: dates, bullets, numbers, verbs, layout. Paste the advert and see which words are missing.

  • 03

    Six layouts, one set of content

    A template only changes the appearance. Switching never loses what you typed — and each one says whether it is safe for a parser.

  • 04

    Your data stays with you

    The site has no backend, so there is nowhere to send it. Your CV lives in browser storage; a JSON file carries it to another computer.

How it works

Four steps, no sign-up.

  1. 1

    Fill in the form

    Contact details, summary, experience, education and skills. The builder opens with an example.

  2. 2

    Choose a layout and colour

    Six templates, six accent colours and a type-size control for when there is more content than room.

  3. 3

    Run the ATS check

    The tab shows a score and specific notes. Paste a job advert to see which keywords are missing.

  4. 4

    Print or save a PDF

    The button opens the print dialog. Choose “Save as PDF” and set margins to none — the sheet carries its own.

Questions and answers

Does my data go anywhere?+

No. The site is a set of static files with no backend — there is nowhere to send it. Your CV is stored only in your browser and disappears when you clear site data. The ATS check also runs locally.

How do I save the CV as a PDF?+

Click “Print / save PDF” and choose the “Save as PDF” destination. Set margins to none — the sheet already carries its own.

How do I know the ATS check is telling the truth?+

Every rule states what it looked for and what it found, so you can disagree with it. It does not simulate one particular system — it is a list of the things parsers most often trip over.

Which template should I pick?+

For applying through a recruitment portal, one marked ATS-safe: Classic, Minimal or Compact. When the CV goes straight to a person, Sidebar, Timeline and Modern look better.

Can I come back to my CV next week?+

Yes. Your work is saved automatically in this browser. To move it to another computer, use “Export JSON” and load the file on the other side.
